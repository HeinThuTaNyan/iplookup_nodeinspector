Scanning your connection telemetry...
1. 🌐 Your Public IP & ISP HIGHLIGHTING

Your internet provider (ISP) assigns your router a Public IP Address. Every website or app you connect to sees this address and uses it to identify your Country, City, and Wi-Fi provider.

2. 🛡️ VPN & Proxy Detection TAP TO VIEW

Look at the highlighted role boxes: if they display VPN DETECTED, PROXY, or DATACENTER, websites can tell you are using a VPN! If it shows RESIDENTIAL [CLEAN], you appear as a normal home user.

3. 🚨 Threat & Hacker Reputation TAP TO VIEW

Public IPs are often shared across thousands of users. We check live security databases (AbuseIPDB & Shodan) to see if your IP was reported for spam, botnets, or hacking—or if it is 0% Clean.

4. 📍 Why the Map is Only an Estimate TAP TO VIEW

A Public IP alone cannot pinpoint your exact house or street! Without GPS permission, an IP lookup only shows the estimated city or central routing building of your internet provider (ISP).
